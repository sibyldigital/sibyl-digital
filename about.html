Deep Dive

The longer story

Creativity has always been the throughline of my life. I grew up immersed in the arts, studying painting, sculpture, and community building in high school through the ArtQuest Fine Arts Program at Santa Rosa High School. I was honored to receive a full scholarship to CSSSA (California State Summer School for the Arts), where I focused on sculpture and painting and deepened my understanding of art as a powerful medium for connection.

In my early twenties, I worked as a yoga teacher, herbalist, and meditation coach, spending over five years in the wellness and lifestyle industry. During that time, I traveled across the country, exploring intentional communities and building hands-on experience in grassroots organizing and collaborative living. I witnessed countless passionate practitioners with incredible offerings struggle to thrive due to a lack of strategic business frameworks. Recognizing that gap, I returned to school in my late twenties to bridge the worlds of creativity, community, and commerce.

I earned my B.S. in Business and Marketing from the University of Arizona Global Campus, graduating in 2024. While studying business management, marketing strategy, and operational planning, I found myself drawn to the intersection of creativity and structure, the space where visual storytelling supports business strategy and strong systems bring ideas to life. That realization led me to focus my career on building brand systems, creative operations, and internal infrastructure that help organizations grow with clarity and cohesion.

I'm passionate about working in in-house, agency, or freelance settings where I can stay aligned with my values: creating systems and experiences that feel good, function smoothly, and support both the business and the people behind it. My focus is on designing not just for aesthetics, but for long-term sustainability, building structures that help offerings thrive authentically in the world.

Today, I bring a unique lens to branding, marketing strategy, and graphic design. I believe that a brand is more than just how it looks, it's how it feels, functions, and flourishes. A business's visual identity must align with its operational practices and strategic goals. Authentic design isn't just beautiful, it's rooted in truth, transparency, and practical action.
